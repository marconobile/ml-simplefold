TAU:
tau controls the amount of random diffusion noise injected at every Euler–Maruyama sampling step. The default is 0.3 ([cli.py (line 141)](/home/nobilm@usi.ch/ml-simplefold/src/simplefold/conditioned_sampling/cli.py:141)).
The stochastic update contains:
\[
\sqrt{2\,\Delta t\,D(t)\,\tau}\;\epsilon
\]where \(D(t)\) is the time-dependent diffusion coefficient and \(\epsilon\) is Gaussian noise ([sampler.py (line 136)](/home/nobilm@usi.ch/ml-simplefold/src/simplefold/model/torch/sampler.py:136)).
Therefore:
tau = 0: deterministic sampling; no random noise is added.
Smaller tau: less stochasticity and more reproducible/similar structures.
Larger tau: more noise and potentially more diverse structures.
Noise amplitude scales as \(\sqrt{\tau}\), so quadrupling tau doubles the injected noise amplitude.
It does not directly control conditioning strength—that is --guidance-scale. tau controls sampler stochasticity while the cluster conditioning still guides the generated structure. The entry script delegates this argument to the conditioned workflow, which passes it into EMSampler ([workflow.py (line 264)](/home/nobilm@usi.ch/ml-simplefold/src/simplefold/conditioned_sampling/workflow.py:264)).
